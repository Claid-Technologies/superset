You give an agent a task, then step away from your desk. A few minutes later, it needs an answer. Or the work is ready to review. Either way, the next step is waiting on you.

Superset Mobile brings that next step to your iPhone.

Open the workspaces you already use on your computer. Send a follow-up prompt, check the terminal, or review what changed. You can keep work moving without opening your laptop.

![Superset Mobile terminal on an iPhone, with the headline Leave your desk. Keep building.](/images/blog/superset-mobile/hero.png)

## Same workspaces. Wherever you are.

Superset Mobile connects you to your workspaces and sessions on your connected machine. Your agents run there; your phone gives you a way to pick up the conversation.

It works with Claude Code, Codex, and other terminal agents you use in Superset. You don't need to start a new session or explain the task again just because you've switched screens.

## Give the next instruction

Check what an agent is doing in its terminal session. Answer its question, clarify the task, or send the next prompt from your phone.

That small interaction can be enough to keep a task moving while you're away from your desk.

## Review the changes

Check pull request status and open syntax-highlighted diffs to see what changed. Read the added and removed lines, then give your agent feedback from the same workspace.

![Superset Mobile showing a syntax-highlighted code diff on an iPhone.](/images/blog/superset-mobile/diffs.png)
